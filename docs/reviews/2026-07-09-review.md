## 2. Findings (priority order)

### F1 · Low · CONFIRMED — grey-gate explanation is misleading for a stock you don't hold
**app.py:18861** (`⚙️ Gate checks on the Trade Plan`)

The text says a grey `—` gate means *"data not yet loaded — check the 💰 Account
page or 🔔 Catalyst Watch page first."* But the gate checklist also renders for a
**non-held** stock you're researching (the `if _sa_holding … / elif … / elif
_sa_holding` chain at app.py:12615-12699 fires no banner for a name you don't
own, and execution falls straight through to the gate block at 12710). For such a
name:
- **Concentration** is grey **by design** — `_gc_tw` stays `None` because the
  ticker isn't in `_port_df_enriched` (app.py:12722-12724). You hold zero of it;
  there is no concentration to check. Opening the Account page will **not** turn
  it green.
- **R/R** renders grey with detail *"R/R unavailable"* when `rr_val` is falsy
  (app.py:12742) — nothing to do with the Account or Catalyst pages the sentence
  points to.

Failure scenario: a user researching a fresh Buy candidate sees grey
Concentration, follows the guide to the Account page, finds nothing to fix, and
concludes the app is broken. Suggest distinguishing "grey because you hold none
of this / it's a fresh entry" from "grey because portfolio/earnings data hasn't
loaded yet."

### F2 · Low · CONFIRMED — "needs the most improvement" contradicts "easiest lever first"
**app.py:18782** (`📊 Understanding verdict changes`)

The sentence reads: *"which of the four pillars … **needs the most improvement**
to close that gap (**sorted easiest lever first**)."* The panel actually shows the
pillars requiring the **least** improvement — `_pillar_triggers.sort(key=lambda
x: x[3])` (ascending gain-needed) then `[:2]` (app.py:12182, 12206). "Needs the
most improvement" and "easiest lever first" point in opposite directions; a
reader expecting the hardest pillar gets the two easiest. Suggest: "which
pillar(s) would most easily close the gap (the smallest lift, shown first)."

### F3 · Low (nit) · PLAUSIBLE — "at the top of the Trade Plan" overstates placement
**app.py:18861** (`⚙️ Gate checks on the Trade Plan`)

The text says the gate row is *"at the top of the Trade Plan."* It actually
renders after the verdict banner, the held-position add banners
(app.py:12615-12699), and the stop-ladder explainer (app.py:12708), immediately
**above the Position Sizing block** (app.py:12746 → 12780). Accurate enough for
guidance, but "just above position sizing" would match what the user sees.
