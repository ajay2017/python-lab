How the labels are worked out (sample rule; the cut-offs are your call)

  • Falling below its 50-day average and behind the S&P 500 by more than X points over 4 weeks, holding for N sessions in a row.
  • Weakening exactly one of those two legs.
  • Rising above its 50-day average and that average is rising.
  • Anything else reads Steady and isn't listed in the dock (it is in the full table).
  • "From high" is the drop from the 52-week high. The words correction (−10%) and bear market (−20%) appear only on the S&P 500 / Nasdaq rows, with those cut-offs in constants.py.

Decisions for you

  1. Q1 Label rule and cut-offs. The rule above, with X and N set by you. Suggested: X = 3 points, N = 3 sessions (stops a label flickering day to day). They go in constants.py, so this is a policy call.
  2. Q2 How loud the Notices line is. Not needed: option 2 dropped 2026-11-21.
  3. Q3 Count Weakening in the Notices line? Not needed: option 2 dropped 2026-11-21.
  4. Q4 Which indices get a trend line. Suggested: S&P 500 and Nasdaq only; the Dow tile stays as today.
  5. Q5 The software fund IGV backs three app sectors (AI & Cloud, AI & Data, Enterprise Tech). Suggested: one row "Software (IGV)" that lists the three, as shown, instead of three identical rows.
  6. Q6 Full table as a dialog or a popover. Suggested: dialog (computed only when opened; a popover would compute on every rerun).
  7. Q7 Morning email. Suggested: not now. The email line comes with option 3 (alert only when a sector changes label), so the email doesn't repeat the same line daily.
  8. Q8 Home for the full table. Decided 2026-11-21: 🌐 Macro, at the top of the page. The 🔥 Sector Heatmap tab (🥧 Portfolio Overview → 📈 Analytics) becomes a one-line pointer, so the app keeps one sector table. The dock's dialog is the same table from the same function.
  9. Q9 Keep the heatmap's 1-week / 3-month / 6-month columns? Suggested: yes, at the right of the table (shown), so nothing the heatmap showed is lost in the move.
  10. Q10 🌐 Macro before 🏠 Home has loaded your book. Today the whole Macro page waits for the portfolio. Suggested: show the trend table anyway (it is market data), with the "Your weight" column reading "open 🏠 Home first" until Home has built. The rest of the page keeps its current wait.

What this does not do

  • No forecast and no probability: every figure describes what has already happened.
  • No change to any pick, add, trim, size or gate. That is option 4 (shadow first) and needs its own design and review.
  • Data: the sector funds are already downloaded for the 📊 Portfolio Analysis heatmap (6 months today). The 52-week high needs that download stretched to 1 year, plus a 1-year Nasdaq history (SPY's already exists).
  • When the data can't be loaded, the dock says so and Notices carries a quiet "not checked" note, never a calm-looking reading (the cache publishes None).
