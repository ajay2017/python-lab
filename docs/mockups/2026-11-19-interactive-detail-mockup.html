InteractiveDetail: popover & dialog mockup

For the owner to OK before anything is built. Five spots, each shown as today next to the proposed version. Click the buttons: the popovers and dialogs work as they would in the app. Figures on the Reports spots are from the October 2026 Monthly Decision Review (generated 2026-11-19); the funnel, histogram and debate figures are illustrative only.

Popover: st.popover

  • A button. A click opens a small floating panel; a click outside closes it.
  • Opening it is client-side: instant, no rerun.
  • Its contents still compute on every run, so it declutters but doesn't speed anything up.
  • Fits: "why / how it's computed" text, a page guide.

Dialog: @st.dialog

  • A button. A click opens a modal over the page.
  • Its body runs only when opened, so a heavy chart stops costing time on every page load.
  • Clicks inside it rerun only the dialog. One dialog open at a time.
  • Fits: per-ticker detail, a chart, a small form.
Never moved: warnings, gate and suppression banners, stale-data notices and the Home ⚠️ Alerts expander stay visible, because the house rule says a suppression must show a visible banner. Moved content keeps 🙈 Hide values masking, the read-only viewer's locks and safe_html, and the same $ / ** renderer rules apply inside a popover.

Acted on = a buy of that name on the call day or within 2 trading days after it (New Position, Add to Winner and Enter Now calls; an Enter Now on a name you already held is left out). 6 name(s) whose every call was still open at the month's end and 0 not judged are in neither count; a name with any closed call is judged on its closed calls. The band chart prices every call from its call-day price; the bought average uses your fill price, up to 2 trading days after the call. 📜 Recommendations History and the other pages that count acted calls still use the older rule (a same-day trade tagged as a recommendation), so their counts can differ.
