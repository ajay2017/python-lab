Option A: coloured cards, one glance per sector

Each sector is a card. Left stripe = what the regime map says (green = usually favoured, red = usually lags). Chip = the actual trend. When they clash, the whole card turns amber with "trend disagrees". Easiest to read; closest to today's layout.
