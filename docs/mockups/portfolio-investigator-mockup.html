Ask an open-ended question about your trading — this composes existing, already-reviewed analysis functions to investigate it, the way a research pass would, rather than looking up one fact. Read-only: never changes a recommendation, a gate, or a trade. Three kinds of questions work today:

• "Is my ~7-day median holding period mechanical or discretionary?"
• "Are the EXIT/TRIM signals actually miscalibrated, or is it a few names?"
• "Does [cluster/sector] earn the risk it's carrying?"
