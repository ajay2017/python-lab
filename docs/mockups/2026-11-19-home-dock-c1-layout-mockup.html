🏠 Home dock C1: two layouts

C1 moves the Act Today line, the Needs-a-look line and the ⚠️ Alerts contents (renamed ⚠️ Notices) and 💹 Live Prices into the bottom dock, so the Portfolio Command Center comes first on Home. Streamlit fixes each tab label and the starting tab when the dock is created, at the top of the page run, before Home has worked out today's counts. So a label like "Act Today 3" can't show this run's number. Switch between the three days below and click the tabs to compare.
