Rules behind the headline
  • Dollars first, with the % beside them. No annualizing until there are 12 months of history.
  • Both sides use the same close: your last trusted end-of-day account record (cash read after the close) and SPY's close that same day. That's why it says "as of Fri Nov 13 close" on a Sunday.
  • "The same money in SPY" = $7,152.99 put into SPY at the Jun 25 close, plus any later deposits on their own dates (none so far).
  • Green = ahead, red = behind. No other judgement words on the headline.
