What changes

  • The index strip, the Act Today bar, Needs a look, ⚠️ Alerts and 💹 Live Prices leave the top of Home and become the dock's four tabs.
  • The Portfolio Command Center is the first thing on Home. On a bad day the protective banners (manual stops unreadable, held-stop basis, data outage) still sit above it and are never folded away.
  • Every tab label carries its count, so "🔴 Act Today 3" is on screen wherever you've scrolled, even with the dock collapsed.
  • From the Command Center down, nothing changes.
