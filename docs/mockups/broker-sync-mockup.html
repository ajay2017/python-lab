Connect your Robinhood account to enable automatic sync
🔗
One-time setup — takes about 2 minutes
DRISHTA connects to Robinhood via SnapTrade (read-only). After setup, balances, positions, and trade history sync automatically each morning.
  1. Sign up at snaptrade.com and create an app to get your CLIENT_ID and CONSUMER_KEY.
  2. Add three Railway variables: SNAPTRADE_CLIENT_ID, SNAPTRADE_CONSUMER_KEY, SNAPTRADE_USER_SECRET.
  3. Click "Connect Robinhood" above — you'll be sent to a SnapTrade portal to log in to Robinhood.
  4. Copy the USER_SECRET returned by SnapTrade into the Railway variable and redeploy.
  5. Done — the daily cron will handle syncing from here.
Read-only access. No trades can be placed through DRISHTA.
