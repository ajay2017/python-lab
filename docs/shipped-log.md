New "🔍 Missed-Opportunity Pattern" section on 📊 Recommendations History → Summary, a sibling to (not nested inside) F-161's existing flat missed-opportunities list — **built from the UNFILTERED `_rh_enriched_all` snapshot, deliberately not the page's status-filtered `_rh_enriched`**, so `distinct_missed()`'s "acted via ANY surfacing" safeguard can't silently break under the Acted-only/Missed-only dropdown (a real data-source bug caught at plan-review stage, not shipped). **A naive statistical rebuild was ruled out during planning** — only 44 distinct tickers have ever surfaced as `new_pick` (account query, 2026-07-24), so a sector/price-band bucketing with a `min_n` gate (mirroring `predictive_analytics.py`'s `by_sector_alpha`/`by_conviction`) would leave most buckets at 0-1 members, the exact wall that sank the original P6. Because every groupable field here is a closed, checkable category, this guard is **stricter** than D1's own free-text quote-matching, not a weaker analog of it (the original plan draft argued the opposite and was FIX-FIRST'd for it). Includes ALL outcomes, not winners-only — a deliberate posture safeguard against becoming a FOMO amplifier — and each pattern renders its real win/dodge/flat mix so it can never read as "these were all winners, buy the next one like it." **Explicitly never cross-references a found pattern against current Grow Today candidates, the watchlist, or held positions** — the one design choice that would turn a retrospective pattern into a disguised forward-looking buy signal; any future join needs its own Opus review.
